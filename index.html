How to Play

  • Press and Hold to aim.
  • Drag left or right to set your trajectory.
  • Release to shoot the bubble.
  • Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.
  • Clear hanging bubbles for bonus points.
  • Don't let the bubbles reach the bottom!
