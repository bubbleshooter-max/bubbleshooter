How to Play

  • Press and Hold to aim.
  • Drag to adjust your trajectory using the aim guide.
  • Release to shoot the bubble.
  • Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.
  • Drop floating bubbles and chain consecutive matches for massive combo points!
  • Survive endless waves that get progressively harder.
